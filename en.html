OwnITime Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · 繁體中文

Your data lives mainly on your own phone. We don't run our own servers to store your data, and we don't sell data. There are no ads, and no tracking or analytics tools.

Who we are

OwnITime ("the app") is developed and maintained by OwnITime, an independent developer based in Taiwan. For the data described below, OwnITime is the data controller.

Privacy questions or requests: ownitime@gmail.com.

What data we handle

1. Content you create

  • Reminders and alarms (title, time, repeat rule, notes, color label)
  • To-dos
  • Period records (see "About health data" below)
  • Workout logs (sessions, sets, rest times, body parts, form notes)

By default these stay only on your phone. They leave the device only if you sign in with Google or use the backup feature.

2. Google account information

Collected only if you choose to sign in. Signing in is optional — all core features (alarms, to-dos, period tracking, workout logs) work without it.

What we get: your email address, display name, profile picture URL and an account ID. We use it for one thing: syncing and backing up your data across your own devices.

3. Voice

When you use "say it to add", the app records what you say at that moment and passes it to the phone's speech recognition service to turn it into text: Google speech recognition on Android, Apple speech recognition on iPhone.

  • Recording happens only when you tap the voice button. The app does not listen in the background.
  • The app does not keep the audio. It is discarded once converted to text.
  • Recognition may run on an online speech service. Without a network connection the phone's on-device recognition is used and the audio never leaves the phone.

4. Shared calendar (optional)

You can share your own events with family, coworkers, friends or a partner. This needs sign-in, and people see your events only after you create a share and approve their request to join.

  • What is uploaded to Firebase: the share's name, your display name, and the time, all-day flag and color of the events you choose to share; with "Full" sharing, also the event title. With "Busy only", titles are not uploaded — they only see which times are taken. Notes, locations, checklists and other content are never uploaded.
  • Join requests: when someone asks to join by QR code or invite code, their display name is kept so you can decide.
  • Who can see it: only people you approved. You can remove anyone or delete the whole share at any time; the shared content in the cloud is deleted with it.
  • Events others share with you are kept only on your phone and are not passed on to anyone.

5. Camera and photos (optional)

The workout "share card" can use a photo as its background. The photo is processed only on the phone (including finding people in it so the text avoids faces) and is never uploaded. It is saved to your gallery only when you tap "Download".

6. Subscriptions and paid themes

Subscriptions and paid themes (one-time purchases such as "Night Shanghai") are handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment; the app never sees your card or payment details. It only asks Google Play whether this account has an active subscription or owns a theme.

7. Phone calendars (optional: "Sync Google Calendar")

Used only if you turn on "Choose calendars to show → Sync Google Calendar" and grant the Calendar permission. This works through the phone's own calendar (Android's Calendar permission), not through a calendar permission on your Google sign-in.

  • Read: lists the calendars already on your phone (for example Google account calendars the system has synced, or holiday calendars) and their events, so you can choose which to show in OwnITime. These events are read and shown on the phone only; the app does not upload them or keep a copy.
  • Write: writes OwnITime events with reminders for the next 14 days (title and time) into a local phone calendar named "OwnITime", so a watch or other device can remind you. This calendar is local; the app does not sync it to any cloud account.
  • Turning the switch off deletes the "OwnITime" calendar. You can also revoke the Calendar permission in system settings at any time.

You can also import .ics calendar files yourself; they are read only on the phone.

About health data (period records)

Period records are health data. We know they are more sensitive than other data, so:

  • They stay only on your phone by default.
  • They are stored in Firebase only if you sign in (for syncing), and included in the backup file in your own Google Drive if you use backup.
  • We don't use them for anything else: no analysis, aggregation, sharing or selling.
  • Period records never appear in a shared calendar; only your events are shared.
  • You can delete single records, or everything at once with "Delete account".

If you don't want period data to leave your phone, don't sign in — period tracking works fully without it.

What we don't do

These are long-term commitments that won't change with the business model:

  • We don't sell your data or trade it with data brokers
  • We never record in the background — only when you tap the voice button
  • We don't use what you write for marketing
  • Health data such as period records is never used for ads or given to advertisers

This describes the current state:

  • No ads
  • No analytics, tracking or profiling tools

The app has subscriptions (see above); they don't change any of the points above. If ads are ever added, we will update this page first and tell you clearly in the app; the four long-term commitments still apply.

Legal bases (EEA and UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the app's features you use, including subscriptions and paid themes.
  • Your consent — for optional features you turn on: Google sign-in and sync, backup, shared calendar, voice input, camera, and phone calendar access. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning the feature off, signing out, revoking the permission, or deleting your account.
  • Explicit consent — for period records (health data) when you choose to sign in and sync them. Without sign-in they stay on your phone and we don't process them.

International transfers

If you sign in, data synced through Firebase / Google Cloud and Google Drive may be stored on Google servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google provides safeguards for these transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry.

Delete one item

Delete it in the app.

Delete everything and your account

Settings → Account & sync → Delete account

This permanently deletes:

  • All your data in Firebase (including shared calendars you created and your requests to join others)
  • All backups the app created in your Google Drive
  • All data on this device
  • Your Google account's sign-in identity in the app

This cannot be undone. To keep a copy, first use "Settings → Data → Export data" to save a JSON file.

Take your data with you

"Settings → Data → Export data" exports one complete JSON file with all your data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we don't knowingly collect children's personal data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can view, correct, export or delete your data at any time inside the app, without contacting us.

EEA and UK: you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete and correct it, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don't use sensitive personal information (such as health data) for anything other than providing the features you use.

To make a request: ownitime@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the "Last updated" date at the top. For significant changes (for example a new third-party service, or a new purpose for your data), we will tell you clearly in the app.

Applicable law

This policy is written under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act. If you are in the EEA, the UK or California, you also have the rights your local law gives you (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), as described above.
